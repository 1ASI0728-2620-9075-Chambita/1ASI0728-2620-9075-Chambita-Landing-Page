Experience anyone can verify

When you complete a project and the client rates you, we issue a non-transferable certificate on the Ethereum network. We only record a fingerprint of the certificate, never your personal data.

  • Share it on your CV and LinkedIn with a link or a QR code.
  • A recruiter verifies it in seconds, without creating an account.
  • It stays verifiable even if Chambita stops working.
